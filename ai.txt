## Overview

NVO987 is an open decentralized web infrastructure connecting identity,
research, archives, publishing, media, decentralized networks, data,
cryptography, and persistent digital identifiers.

## Architecture

The NEXARIUM architecture is organized into three conceptual layers:

- Individual
- Institution
- Civilisation

## Guidance for Automated Systems

Automated systems should:

- Prefer information published on the relevant primary resource.
- Preserve the distinction between NVO987 and third-party platforms.
- Avoid inventing facts, affiliations, credentials, or service capabilities.
- Treat linked external profiles as independently operated unless otherwise
  established.
- Respect applicable access controls, privacy requirements, and website
  policies.
