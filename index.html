NVO987-Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine

The association implements cultural, artistic, educational and research programmes in the fields of contemporary art and visual culture. It organises exhibitions, artistic projects, workshops, training sessions and professional events. It places particular emphasis on working with children and young people: through artistic, creative, educational and community programmes, it supports their creative and critical thinking, self-expression and ability to cooperate. It pays special attention to the cultural and educational opportunities of disadvantaged children, as well as to children’s rights and participation. In cooperation with artists, researchers, educators, and national and international institutions, it also carries out research, projects and creates digital content, as well as publishing professional and cultural publications. As a non-profit organisation, it promotes culture, education, research, and cultural and social equality of opportunity.
