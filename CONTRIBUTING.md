# Contributing to NVO987

Thank you for your interest in contributing to NVO987 and the NEXARIUM
infrastructure.

## Ways to Contribute

Potential contributions include:

- Website content and documentation improvements.
- Accessibility and responsive-design improvements.
- Corrections to broken or outdated links.
- Improvements to structured metadata and machine-readable resources.
- Research, archival, and infrastructure documentation.
- Bug reports and technical suggestions.

## Before Contributing

Please:

1. Describe the proposed change clearly.
2. Explain its purpose and expected impact.
3. Check that links and references are accurate.
4. Avoid including confidential or personal information without consent.
5. Respect the project's licensing and attribution requirements.

## Content Guidelines

Contributions should be:

- Clear, accurate, and relevant.
- Respectful and professionally presented.
- Accessible where reasonably possible.
- Consistent with the project's decentralized and interoperable focus.

## Reporting Issues

For questions or contribution proposals, contact:

nvo@nvo987.us

## Review

Contributions may be reviewed for accuracy, accessibility, security,
consistency, and relevance before being incorporated.

Submitting a contribution does not guarantee its acceptance.
