# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NVO987 aims to support an open, respectful, and constructive environment
for collaboration, research, cultural activity, and the exchange of ideas.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Communicate constructively and in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Provide accurate information and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual property.
- Contribute to an environment free from harassment and discrimination.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of collaborative activity.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Knowingly misrepresenting another person's work or statements.

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct related to NVO987 should be directed to:

nvo@nvo987.us

Reports should include enough information to understand the concern.
Personal information should only be included when necessary.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in NVO987-related projects,
communications, and collaborative activities.
