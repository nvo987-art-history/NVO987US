# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting
NVO987, please report it responsibly.

Contact:

nvo@nvo987.us

Include, where possible:

- A concise description of the suspected vulnerability.
- The affected domain, endpoint, or component.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Potential impact.
- Any relevant technical details.

## Responsible Disclosure

Please:

- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that does not belong to you.
- Do not disrupt services or degrade their availability.
- Do not publish details of an unresolved vulnerability without coordination.
- Do not collect or disclose personal information unnecessarily.

## Scope

Potentially relevant assets include:

- https://nvo987.us/
- https://identity.nvo987.us
- NVO987-related subdomains and infrastructure.

The inclusion of a domain here does not imply that every linked third-party
service is operated or controlled by NVO987.

## Response

Reports will be reviewed as circumstances permit. A response timeline
or remediation commitment is not guaranteed.

## Safe Harbour

Security research must comply with applicable law and the terms of the
systems being tested. Do not assume authorization to test third-party
services linked from the website.
