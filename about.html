NVO987 — INDIVIDUAL

Throughout my professional career, I have gained professional experience and knowledge in the fields of communication, project management, information technology, law, finance, as well as children's development, education, rights, and support. I have led and implemented numerous European Union-funded projects, primarily focusing on long-term community value creation, sustainable cultural development, and the development of new forms of collaboration. My current professional and research activities lie at the intersection of visual culture, algorithmic image environments, and AI-native web systems. My theoretical work is based on art historical and visual cultural foundations, with particular attention to critical and decolonial approaches, transnational artistic dialogue, as well as questions related to the perception of color and embodied experience. My long-term research examines how algorithmically generated and digitally mediated visual environments shape children's visual perception, emotional sensitivity, and processes of meaning-making. My work related to this research direction is documented in several publications, preprints, working papers, and books with DOI and ISBN identifiers. These include the art historical research work "Color as a Generative Principle in the Work of Sonia Delaunay", "Children’s Color Acquisition Across Cultural Environments in the Age of Algorithms", as well as "The Role of Color in Pedagogical and Learning Environments and Children’s Perception". Social responsibility and community engagement are an important part of my work. As a volunteer, I participate in initiatives that support children, with particular attention to social inclusion and expanding opportunities for younger generations. Working with children and supporting children's development is, for me, an interdisciplinary field that connects several areas of knowledge and requires continuous learning.
